About Formula Finder

Formula Finder is a simple tool I made to help find the right formula when you know certain variables. It contains formulas from physics and physical chemistry, covering Danish gymnasium to first-year university level. 📚

How to use it: Enter your known values as either Symbols or Units in the input fields. You can type LaTeX directly or pick from the symbol buttons. The tool will then show you any matching formulas. ⚡

Note: If nothing shows up, try removing some inputs - the formula you're looking for might not use all the variables you entered. 💡
